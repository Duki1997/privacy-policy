# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 4, 2026

## Introduction

Thank you for using **Easy Notes** ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Information We Collect

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

All notes and audio recordings created with the App are stored locally on your device.

## Microphone Permission

The App requests access to the device's microphone solely to allow you to create audio notes.

The microphone is used only when you choose to record audio. Audio recordings remain on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared.

## Data Storage

All data, including:

* Text notes
* Audio notes
* App preferences

is stored locally on your device.

The App does not provide cloud synchronization or online backup services.

## Data Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or disclose your data to third parties.

## Analytics and Advertising

The App does not use:

* Analytics services
* Advertising SDKs
* Tracking technologies
* Crash reporting services

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are responsible for securing your device and maintaining backups if needed.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
